🌸 Privacy Policy — Alphabloom

Developer: SB Gaming  |  Audience: Children aged 3 to 8 & Families  |  Last Updated: November 2026

1. Zero Personal Data Collection

Alphabloom is an offline-capable kids' educational game designed for children aged 3 to 8. We do not collect, store, share, or sell any personally identifiable information (such as names, email addresses, phone numbers, photos, location, or account credentials) from children or parents.

2. Local On-Device Storage Only

Game progress—including selected language, earned star points, daily streak, favorite stories, and chosen voice preference—is saved strictly on your own device using browser localStorage. This data never leaves your device and can be cleared anytime by clearing app data or uninstalling the app.

3. Offline Audio & Text-to-Speech

All background music and sound effects are generated locally on-device. Voice narration uses your device's built-in Speech Synthesis engine and does not record audio or access your microphone.

4. Family-Safe Advertising (Google Play Families Policy)

When online and when ads are enabled, Alphabloom enforces child-directed, non-personalized advertising (CHILD_DIRECTED_TREATMENT: true / npa=1) compliant with the Google Play Families Policy. Banner placements are kept outside active mini-games, break ads use a strict 3-minute cooldown, and optional rewarded bonus ads are protected behind a Parental Gate. All ad spaces are hidden automatically when offline.

5. Third-Party Links & Children's Safety

Alphabloom does not include social media logins, public chat rooms, or in-app purchases for children.

6. Contact & Updates

If you are a parent or guardian with questions about this Privacy Policy, you may contact the developer (SB Gaming) via the developer contact email listed on the Google Play Store listing for Alphabloom.
